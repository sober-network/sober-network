How it works

Your group online in three steps.

A trusted servant sets up the group. Members join privately. Everyone stays connected — no tech expertise required.

01
A trusted servant creates the group
An admin registers the home group with a private slug. They set whether new members require approval — group conscience decides (T2). Admin is a trusted servant role, not a ruler.
02
Members join with a private invite link
Members register with first name only. Last name, phone, sobriety date, and location are always optional — never required. One account can belong to multiple groups (T3).
03
Stay connected between meetings
Access the phone list, check the meeting schedule, read announcements, and reach out to other members — all inside a private, members-only group space.

Design Principles

Three unbreakable commitments.

Not policies. Not settings. Hard-coded architectural constraints.

Anonymity is non-negotiable
No public profiles. No member directories. No searchable rosters. Display names default to first name only. T11 and T12 are enforced at the query layer — not just the UI.
Groups stay autonomous
Every group is its own island. Members of Group A cannot see Group B's phone list, members, or announcements. T4 is enforced at the query layer with mandatory group_id filters.
No engagement mechanics
No likes, streaks, leaderboards, or notifications engineered to pull you back. Our primary purpose is carrying the message, not maximizing time on site (T5).

Not suggestions — the foundations A.A. has found essential for unity and survival. Every feature, query, and design decision on this platform is evaluated against them.
