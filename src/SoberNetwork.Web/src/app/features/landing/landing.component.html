How it works

Your group online in three steps.

A trusted servant sets up the group. Members join privately. Everyone stays connected — no tech expertise required.

01
A trusted servant creates the group
An admin registers the home group with a private slug. They set whether new members require approval — group conscience decides (T2). Admin is a trusted servant role, not a ruler.
02
Members join with a private invite link
Members register with first name only. Last name, phone, sobriety date, and location are always optional — never required. One account can belong to multiple groups (T3).
03
Stay connected between meetings
Access the phone list, check the meeting schedule, read announcements, and reach out to other members — all inside a private, members-only group space.

Principles

Built around the 12 Traditions.

The 12 Traditions of A.A. guide everything we build. Anonymity (T11/T12) and group autonomy (T4) are non-negotiable architectural constraints.

T11 No public member directories. First names only by default.
T4 Each group is autonomous. Data never leaks between groups.
T5 No advertising, sponsorships, or engagement mechanics.
T7 Fully self-supporting. Free for home groups, forever.
